# BUSINESS — Bex

> The business at a glance. The essentials I always keep in mind.

## What the business is
Bex Fit — online fitness coaching for busy mums. Makes money through 1-to-1 online coaching and a small group program.

## Revenue
~$12K/month.

## How the week goes
Mornings: client check-ins and program writing. Midday: content. Afternoons: DMs, sales calls, admin. Evenings: more DMs.

## Biggest time-drain
Following up leads and answering the same questions in DMs over and over.

## Team
Solo — just Bex.

## Tools & platforms
- Payments: Stripe
- Client delivery: Trainerize
- Funnels/site: Kajabi
- Email/CRM: Kajabi email
- Scheduling: Calendly
- Content/social: Instagram
- Other: Google Sheets for tracking

## 12-month goal
100 clients, less time in DMs, and a self-paced "Strong Mum" app launched.
