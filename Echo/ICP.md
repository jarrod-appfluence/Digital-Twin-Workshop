# ICP — Bex's dream client

> Who we serve. The sharper this is, the better the content and copy I write.

## Who they are
Mums aged 32-44 with 1-3 young kids, working part-time or full-time, who used to be fit and active before kids and feel like they've lost themselves.

## The 2am fear
That she's let herself go for good and her kids will only ever know the tired, unfit version of her — and that she'll never get her old body or energy back.

## What they've already tried
Fad diets, F45/BodyFit memberships she stopped using, MyFitnessPal, random YouTube workouts, "starting Monday" over and over.

## What they actually want
To feel like herself again — strong, confident, energetic — to stop hiding in photos, and to set an example for her kids.

## Where to find them
Instagram (follows other mum-fitness accounts), local mums' Facebook groups, school pickup networks. Usually finds Bex via Reels and referrals.

## Bad fit
Comp athletes, 20-something gym bros, anyone wanting a quick crash-diet fix, people who won't commit 12 weeks.
